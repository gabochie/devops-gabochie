Privacy

Short version: this site keeps no analytics and no tracking cookies.

No analytics, no trackers, no third-party ads. Two things live only in your own browser: your theme choice (localStorage) and, only if you open the private consoles, a device cookie that unlocks them for 30 days on that device. Neither is sent anywhere. The service worker caches public pages for offline reading only. The admin console fetches public repository statistics; nothing you do there is recorded. If you email me, your message lives in my inbox and nowhere else — I will never sell, share, or publish client information, and client work is only ever shown publicly with explicit permission.

If you use the 60-second readiness check, your answers and email go to my mailing provider (Brevo) solely to deliver your assessment and one follow-up. Ask for deletion anytime at devops@gabochie.com and it's gone.
