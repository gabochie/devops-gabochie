Engagement terms

1. Fixed quotes. Every engagement starts with a free scoping call and a written fixed fee. No hourly billing, no work without your explicit approval per item.

2. Audit-first. Read-only inspection comes before any change. You own the findings report either way.

3. Staging before production. No production change without a verified staging result and rehearsed rollback.

4. Secrets. I work with secret names, never values. Leaked live keys are rotated with you immediately.

5. Honesty. Remaining risks are listed, not hidden. No invented testimonials, metrics, or guarantees — only verifiable evidence.

6. Payment. Every engagement starts with a written invoice — never a surprise charge. USD invoices settle by Wise or Payoneer; GHS invoices by Paystack, Mobile Money or bank transfer. Payment details are emailed with the invoice; the client portal shows invoice status whenever checkout is enabled for your project.

7. Delivery. The $49 Risk Scan report (findings table + video) lands within 24 hours of confirmed payment. Audits run 1–3 days, hardening sprints 1–2 weeks, both confirmed in writing before start. If a date I committed to slips, tell me: the undelivered portion is refundable in full, or we re-scope — your choice.

8. Refunds. Read-only reports are yours either way, delivered or not (if not delivered, full refund). Fixed-fee projects: full refund before work starts; after start, pro-rata for undelivered items only. Email devops@gabochie.com and it's handled in writing.
