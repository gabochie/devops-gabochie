FAQ

Will you rewrite my app?

No. Preserve-what-works is the rule: smallest reversible diffs, public interfaces unchanged unless evidence demands it.

How do you charge?

Fixed price for a scoped project, or a weekly retainer for ongoing work. Quoted upfront and confirmed in writing before anything starts.

Are you open to full-time?

Remote freelance for now, with room to grow into a longer engagement. If you need someone embedded part-time and reliable, start there.

What do you need from me?

Repo access, a staging environment (or permission to create one), and 30 minutes for scoping. I never need your production secrets — names only.

Which stacks?

Next.js, Node APIs, Python, PHP/Laravel, WordPress, Cloudflare edge, static sites — with Supabase or Neon Postgres, D1, and Docker in the mix. If it's vibe-coded and on the web, I can audit it.

How do I pay?

USD invoices settle by Wise or Payoneer. GHS invoices settle by Paystack or Mobile Money. Invoice first, fixed scope, receipt on completion.

How do I know it worked?

The same gates re-run after every fix, on staging first. Green is demonstrated, not declared — and remaining risks are listed, not hidden.

Do you work outside Ghana?

Yes — most of my work is remote for teams in Europe and North America. UTC+0 gives a full EU overlap and US-East mornings.
